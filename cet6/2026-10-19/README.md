### ① 选词填空（26—35题）

主题：**AI 与人类创造力、工作价值的变化**。

- 答案：**26 I · 27 B · 28 M · 29 C · 30 K · 31 A · 32 D · 33 F · 34 J · 35 O**
- 重点词汇：*cognitive, domains, surpass, redundant, accompanying, entertaining, hybrid, oversee, vanish*。
- 补充备选词：*evoking, impetus, intuitive, stances, tentatively*。
- 解题方法：先判断空格所需词性，再结合并列结构、词组搭配和上下文逻辑选词。
- 重要语法：现在分词作后置定语、并列谓语、形容词并列、cause + 宾语 + to do。

### ② 仔细阅读 Passage Two（51—55题）

主题：**如何理解并应对别人不请自来的建议**。

- 答案：**51 D · 52 B · 53 D · 54 A · 55 C**
- 重点词汇：*unsolicited, well-meaning, vulnerability, validate, excessive, recipient, manipulative, perceive, perspective, compassion, boundaries*。
- 重要短语：*under the guise of, accuse sb. of doing sth., perceive ... as, double down on, get by, take ... into consideration, take into account*。
- 长难句重点：

  > You can let them know that you have heard them and appreciate where they are coming from without taking on the potentially damaging narrative that you could not have gotten by without their help.

  重点理解：**宾语从句 that、without + doing、同位语从句 that、where someone is coming from、get by**。
- 阅读核心：寻找原文定位句并识别同义替换，理解作者对“温和但坚定地设立边界”的建议。
